# YZCONN – Wire Harness and Connector Manufacturer

## Company Overview

YZCONN is the international brand of Yz-Link Technology Co., Ltd., a manufacturer specializing in custom wire harnesses, cable assemblies, and connectors.

Founded in 2004, Yz-Link Technology provides OEM and ODM manufacturing solutions for industrial equipment manufacturers, automotive suppliers, and electronics companies.

## Engineering and Manufacturing Capabilities

YZCONN provides integrated engineering and manufacturing services, including:

- Custom wire harness design and development
- Connector design and manufacturing
- Cable assembly and OEM production
- Engineering review and DFM assessment
- Prototype development and sample validation
- Tooling and injection molding
- Electrical testing and quality inspection
- Mass production and long-term supply

## Quality Management

YZCONN emphasizes manufacturing process control, product reliability, and quality verification.

Its manufacturing and inspection processes include incoming material inspection, in-process quality control, electrical testing, and final inspection.

## Applications

YZCONN products are used in:

- Industrial automation
- Automotive electronics
- Medical equipment
- Robotics and drones
- Communication equipment
- Home appliances
- New energy systems
